    What is Aperture?

    Studievereniging Aperture is the official study association of the Maastricht Science Programme (MSP), part of the Faculty of Science and Engineering (FSE) at Maastricht University. Founded on 6 November 2012, Aperture provides study-oriented and recreational activities to MSP students, as well as other student-oriented services.
